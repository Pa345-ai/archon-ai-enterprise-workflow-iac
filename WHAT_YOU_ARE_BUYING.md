# What You Are Buying

This document provides a plain-English summary of the product, its purpose, and its value proposition. It is intended for a non-technical audience, including business stakeholders, procurement officers, and legal teams.

---

## 1. The Product

You are purchasing a **Reusable Institutional Cloud Infrastructure Foundation**.

Think of it as a pre-built, expert-level blueprint for running a secure and reliable application on the Amazon Web Services (AWS) cloud. Instead of spending 6-12 months and hundreds of thousands of dollars on custom engineering and security reviews, you are acquiring a ready-made foundation that has been designed to meet the standards of a regulated industry.

This is **not a software application**. It is the infrastructure *on which* you will run your own custom applications.

## 2. The Core Value Proposition

The primary value of this product is **speed to market** and **risk reduction**.

*   **Speed:** It allows your development teams to start building your application on a secure, production-ready environment on day one. It dramatically shortens the timeline for achieving a compliant and scalable cloud presence.
*   **Risk Reduction:** The architecture has been designed by enterprise-level cloud architects and security professionals. It comes with built-in security controls, compliance documentation, and operational best practices, significantly reducing the risk of a security breach, data loss, or a failed audit.

## 3. What is Included in the One-Time License Fee?

Your purchase includes a perpetual license to the following:

*   **A Complete Infrastructure Blueprint:** The full Terraform codebase to deploy the architecture.
*   **Enterprise-Grade Documentation:** A full suite of documents that prove the architecture was professionally designed and is ready for enterprise use. This includes:
    *   **Architecture Decision Records (ADRs):** Explaining *why* the system is built the way it is.
    *   **Operational Runbooks:** A "survival guide" for your engineering team to manage the infrastructure.
    *   **Compliance Matrix:** Evidence to show your auditors that the infrastructure meets common security standards.
*   **Commercial and Legal Guarantees:** Clear documents defining the scope of the product, its limitations, and your rights as a buyer.

## 4. What is NOT Included?

*   **Your Application Code:** You are responsible for building and deploying your own software.
*   **Managed Services:** We do not run this infrastructure for you. Your team is responsible for its ongoing operation.
*   **AWS Costs:** You are responsible for the monthly bill from Amazon for the cloud resources that are deployed.

In simple terms, you are buying the architectural plans and the foundational materials for a world-class factory. You are still responsible for building your products inside the factory and paying the electricity bill.
